SampleDir Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-17

Disclaimer. This document is a template provided for convenience and does not constitute legal advice. Have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer licensed in your jurisdiction before publishing or relying on it.

1. Our Principle: Local-First

SampleDir is a local-first desktop app. Your sample files, audio content, and folder structures stay on your device. We do not upload them to any cloud.

2. What We Collect

  • License / activation data: a device fingerprint (used only to enforce the device limit), activation status, and — if provided through checkout — your email. Processed via our license backend and Creem.
  • Anonymous usage analytics (optional): page views and button clicks on our website, collected via a privacy-respecting script. No personally identifiable information.
  • Crash logs (optional, only if you choose to send them): non-personal diagnostics to help us fix bugs.

3. What We Do NOT Collect

  • The contents of your audio files or sample libraries.
  • File paths or metadata of your personal media beyond what is needed for activation.
  • Any cloud backup of your data.

4. How We Use Data

To validate your license, provide support, prevent abuse (for example, key sharing), and improve the product.

5. Payments

Payments are processed by Creem (Merchant of Record). We never receive or store your full card number.

6. Data Retention

Activation records are kept for as long as your license is active. You may request deletion (see §8).

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (for example, GDPR / CCPA), you may access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, email hhztiger@gmail.com.

8. International Transfers

If you are outside [YOUR COUNTRY], your activation data may be processed there. We rely on our license backend and Creem for any cross-border handling.

9. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website uses minimal local storage for language preference. The app stores settings locally on your device only.

10. Third Parties

  • Cloudflare — CDN / download delivery.
  • Creem — payments & license issuance.

11. Changes

We may update this Policy; the latest version is always at this URL.
